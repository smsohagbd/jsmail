How it works: When the delivery engine picks a message destined for a domain on this list, it immediately marks all matching recipients as suppressed and moves on — without opening a TCP connection, performing any DNS/MX lookup, or consuming a retry slot. The send log entry is preserved so you have a record of what was blocked.
Examples: gmail.com   yahoo.com   hotmail.com   — exact recipient domain match (not a subdomain wildcard).
